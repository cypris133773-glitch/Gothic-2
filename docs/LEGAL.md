# Originality

GRIMWARD is an original game in the tradition of the German open-world RPGs of
the early 2000s, *Gothic II: Night of the Raven* (Piranha Bytes, 2003) above all.
It is a homage, not a port, not a remake, and not a copy.

**What it takes from that tradition:** design. Systems, mechanics, progression
math, pacing, the philosophy of a hand-placed world with no level scaling, the
shape of a stat threshold that gates a weapon, factions that lock each other
out, chapter transitions that rewrite the world, NPCs on daily routines. Game
mechanics are not protected by copyright, and reproducing a *design idea* well
is the entire point of the project.

**What it does not take, and will never take:** assets, meshes, textures,
sounds, music, voice lines, scripts, dialogue, map geometry, quest text, or
character and place names. No file from any Gothic release or mod has been
decompiled, extracted, converted or consulted as data. Every asset in this
repository is generated in code from source in this repository.

**Every proper noun here is original.** The mapping between the concepts we
borrowed and the names we invented is in `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, kept current in the
same commit as any new name.

Numbers taken from community documentation of the original (learning-point
costs, the melee damage formula, the experience curve) are cited where they are
used and carry a confidence label — `[V]` corroborated, `[C]` single source,
`[D]` our own decision. They are facts about a well-studied system, recorded the
way a wiki records them, and they are re-derived in our own code.
